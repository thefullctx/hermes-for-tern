# Code of conduct

Hermes for Tern is a small community project. Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions
about the work.

- Be respectful of people and their time, whatever their experience or background.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or unwelcome personal attention.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what is merged, and that a "no" is about fit, not about you.

If something happens that makes this space unwelcome, contact the maintainer,
[@thefullctx](https://github.com/thefullctx), privately on GitHub. Behaviour that breaks these
rules may lead to comments being removed or to a block from the repository.
